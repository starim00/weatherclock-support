PhotoSweep Privacy Policy
PhotoSweep 隐私政策

Effective / 更新日期：2026-07-25

PhotoSweep 无需注册账号，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，不出售个人信息。PhotoSweep does not require an account, contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information.

Face Data / 人脸数据

PhotoSweep does not collect or process face data. The current version does not detect faces, recognize people, identify or compare individuals, create biometric templates, or calculate face-quality scores. No face data or face-derived value is generated, stored, retained, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

PhotoSweep 不收集或处理人脸数据。当前版本不检测人脸、不识别人像、不进行身份识别或比对、不创建生物特征模板，也不计算人脸质量分数。应用不会生成、保存、保留、传输或向第三方共享任何人脸数据或人脸派生值。

Photos and Videos / 照片与视频

在用户通过 iOS 系统权限弹窗授权后，PhotoSweep 才会访问照片图库，用于查找重复或相似照片、检测模糊照片和截图、计算非生物特征的照片质量指标，以及发现可能包含敏感信息的图片。

PhotoSweep accesses the photo library only after authorization through the iOS system prompt. Access is used to find duplicate or similar photos, detect blurry photos and screenshots, calculate non-biometric image-quality metrics, and identify images that may contain sensitive information.

  • 照片、视频和缩略图不会上传到开发者服务器。Photos, videos, and thumbnails are never uploaded to a developer server.
  • 分析通过 Apple 系统框架在设备上完成。Analysis is performed on-device using Apple system frameworks.
  • OCR 原文、证件号码、银行卡号、验证码和二维码内容只在分析时短暂存在于内存，不会保存或写入日志。Recognized text and barcode payloads are transient in memory and are not stored or logged.

Local Results and Retention / 本地结果与保留期限

应用可能在设备上保存照片资源标识、尺寸、非生物特征质量分数、重复分组和经过概括的敏感类别。这些结果仅用于恢复扫描进度和展示整理结果，不包含照片副本、OCR 原文或二维码内容。

The app may store asset identifiers, dimensions, non-biometric quality scores, duplicate groups, and redacted sensitivity categories on the device. These results restore scan progress and display organization results; they do not include photo copies, OCR source text, or barcode contents.

本地结果会保留到用户在 PhotoSweep 设置中重置扫描数据、相关照片被移除或应用被卸载。Local results remain until the user resets scan data in PhotoSweep Settings, the related photo is removed, or the app is uninstalled.

Deletion and Permission Control / 删除与权限控制

用户可以在 PhotoSweep 设置中重置扫描数据，也可以随时在“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片”中修改或撤销照片权限。PhotoSweep 仅在用户明确选择项目并确认后请求系统隐藏或删除照片。

Users can reset scan data in PhotoSweep Settings and can change or revoke photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. PhotoSweep requests hiding or deletion only after the user selects items and confirms the action.

Sharing and Third Parties / 共享与第三方

PhotoSweep 不向开发者或第三方传输照片内容、扫描结果、使用记录、人脸数据或其他生物特征数据。购买和恢复购买由 Apple App Store 处理；应用只读取 Apple 提供的购买权益状态。

PhotoSweep does not transmit photo content, scan results, usage records, face data, or other biometric data to the developer or any third party. Purchases and restoration are handled by the Apple App Store; the app reads only the entitlement status provided by Apple.

Contact / 联系方式

隐私或技术支持问题可通过 PhotoSweep Support Request 提交。提交时请勿附加私人照片、证件号码或付款信息。

Privacy and support questions can be submitted through PhotoSweep Support Request. Do not attach private photos, identity numbers, or payment information.
