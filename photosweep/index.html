快速排查 / Troubleshooting

无法读取照片 / Photos are unavailable

打开“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → PhotoSweep”，选择“完全访问”。如果此前拒绝过授权，PhotoSweep 会提供前往系统设置的入口。

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoSweep and select Full Access. If access was previously denied, PhotoSweep provides a link to system Settings.

扫描没有包含全部照片 / Some photos are missing

有限访问只会包含已选择的照片。仅存于 iCloud 的项目需要先下载到设备；PhotoSweep 会在下载前征求确认。

Limited Access includes only selected photos. iCloud-only items must be downloaded to the device, and PhotoSweep asks before downloading them.

恢复永久版 / Restore the lifetime purchase

在 PhotoSweep 的“设置”页点按“恢复购买”。购买由 App Store 和当前 Apple Account 管理，开发者不会接触付款资料。

Tap Restore Purchases in PhotoSweep Settings. Purchases are managed by the App Store and the current Apple Account; the developer does not receive payment details.

权限与隐私 / Permission & Privacy

首次使用照片功能时，点按“继续”后会显示 iOS 的系统照片权限请求。所有照片分析均在设备上完成，照片和视频不会上传到开发者服务器。

When a photo feature is first used, tapping Continue presents the iOS system photo permission request. All photo analysis is performed on the device; photos and videos are not uploaded to a developer server.

PhotoSweep 不执行人脸检测、识别、身份比对或人脸评分，也不生成、保存或共享人脸数据。

PhotoSweep does not detect, recognize, identify, compare, or score faces and does not generate, store, or share face data.
